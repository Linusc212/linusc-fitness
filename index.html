The Method

Three pillars. One transformation.

I don't just write workouts. I coach the whole person: training, lifestyle, and the mindset that makes it all stick.

01

Fitness

Customised training programmes designed in 4 to 6 week blocks with progressive overload built in. Whether you train at your condo gym, at home, or at a commercial gym, every session has purpose.

02

Lifestyle

No crash diets or rigid meal plans. I help you build sustainable daily habits around eating, sleep, and recovery, one at a time, until your lifestyle supports your goals without taking over your life.

03

Mindset

Consistency beats intensity. Through weekly reflection prompts, accountability check-ins, and real conversation, I help you build the mental habits that keep you going long after motivation fades.

About

Hey, I'm Linus.

I'm a certified personal trainer based in Singapore, and I believe fitness should be built around your life, not the other way around.

I work with busy adults who want to get stronger, feel better, and build habits that actually last. My approach combines evidence-based training, sustainable lifestyle habits, and the mindset coaching that makes it all stick.

Whether I'm meeting you at your condo gym or coaching you online from wherever you are, my job is the same: help you show up consistently, train with purpose, and feel proud of the body you're building.
